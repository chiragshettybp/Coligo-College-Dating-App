# 💙 Coligo

*A modern, secure, and student-first dating platform built exclusively for college communities.*

Coligo helps verified college students discover, match, and connect with people from their campus in a safe and familiar environment. It keeps the experience simple, fast, and focused on meaningful student connections.

---

## 📖 Overview

Coligo is a college-exclusive dating and social discovery platform designed for students.

Instead of connecting with random people, users meet verified students from their own campus or nearby colleges, making conversations more relevant, comfortable, and trustworthy.

---

## ❗ The Problem

Finding genuine connections online has become increasingly difficult.

Many dating platforms are filled with fake profiles, inactive users, and people outside a user's community. Students often want a safer and more relatable space where they can meet people who share the same campus life, interests, and experiences.

Coligo was created to solve this problem with a verified, student-focused experience.

---

## 💡 Our Solution

Coligo creates a trusted environment where college students can discover, match, and chat with other verified students.

By focusing only on college communities, the platform encourages authentic interactions, reduces fake accounts, and makes meeting new people feel more natural and comfortable.

---

## ✨ Key Features

* 🎓 Verified college student community.
* ❤️ Swipe to like and discover new people.
* 💬 Instant chat after a mutual match.
* 📷 Create rich profiles with photos and interests.
* 🏫 Explore students from your campus.
* 🔒 Safety tools including report, block, and unmatch.
* 🔔 Real-time notifications for matches and messages.
* ⚡ Fast onboarding designed for mobile users.
* 🌟 Clean and distraction-free user experience.
* 📈 Built to grow with new social features over time.
